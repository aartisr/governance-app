# Bill Intelligence

The Bills workspace is the starting point for a review. It offers a sortable, searchable collection of legislative records, including sponsor, policy domain, status, and section count.

Open [Bills](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/bills) to begin. Selecting a bill reveals section-level summaries, affected-population context, budget deltas, and the evidence actually linked to that bill.

## Reading a bill responsibly

- Treat summaries as a navigation aid, not a replacement for statutory text.
- Check source coverage before treating a model output as settled.
- Use [Impact analysis](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/impact) to test how a specific district profile changes the picture.
