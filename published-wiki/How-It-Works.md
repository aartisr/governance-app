# How It Works

This is a guide to the current prototype. All displayed civic records and scores are sample data.

| Step | What you can do | What the result means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bills](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/bills) | Search three fictional bills and read section summaries | A demonstration record, not statutory text |
| [Impact](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/impact) | Switch among three sample districts | Formula-based exposure equivalent, not a forecast |
| [Voter feedback](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/feedback) | Adjust priority sliders | A page-local exercise; no vote is collected |
| [Compromise](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/compromise) | Compare four preset amendments | Ranking under assumed utilities and risks |
| [Trust](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/trust) | Inspect sample participant scores | Display context; scores do not affect the recommendation |

The workflow is exploratory. Feedback does not flow into the amendment calculation, group boundaries are not enforced, and the app does not track enacted outcomes. Read the [worked example](https://github.com/aartisr/governance-app/blob/main/docs/how-the-governance-app-works.md) for more context.
