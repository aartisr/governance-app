# Bill Intelligence

The [Bills workspace](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/bills) lets you search, filter, compare, and open three fictional bill records. A detail page shows stored section summaries, budget figures, and sample source cards.

The cards do not link to verified primary documents. Their publisher names, excerpts, and scores are demonstration data. For a real bill, first check current text, status, and amendments with an authoritative legislative source.
