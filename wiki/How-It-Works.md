# How It Works

Pareto Governance Engine connects five decision questions in a single, inspectable workflow:

1. What does the bill say?
2. What evidence supports the interpretation?
3. What is the local impact?
4. How strongly do stakeholder groups care?
5. Which option improves shared outcomes without abandoning essential constraints?

The result is decision support, not automated government. People remain responsible for the choices; the product makes assumptions, sources, and trade-offs easier to evaluate.

## Workflow

| Step | Workspace | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review | [Bills](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/bills) | Structured bill and evidence context |
| Understand | [Impact](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/impact) | District-level estimates and confidence |
| Prioritize | [Voter feedback](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/feedback) | Visible preference intensity |
| Compare | [Compromise analysis](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/compromise) | Options ranked by benefit, support, and risk |
| Verify | [Stakeholder trust](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/trust) | Credibility context beside public input |
