# Voter Feedback and Trust

The feedback workspace makes preference intensity visible by asking users to allocate a limited weekly token budget. Larger signals cost more, making trade-offs explicit.

Open [Voter feedback](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/feedback) to set issue priorities.

Stakeholder trust is separate from preference. Trust scores provide transparent context about accuracy, expertise, consistency, and transparency; they do not silence public input.

Open [Stakeholder trust](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/trust) to review credibility dimensions.
