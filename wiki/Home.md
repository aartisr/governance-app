# Civic Accord

Civic Accord is an evidence-based civic decision-support workspace. It helps people review legislation, understand local policy impact, express priorities, compare compromise options, and evaluate stakeholder trust.

> [Open the live governance workspace](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/)

## An Open Invitation

Democracy is not something done for us. It is something we keep alive by taking part.

Every bill touches real lives. A school, a clinic, a street, a family budget, a small business, or the possibility a young person carries into adulthood can all be shaped by decisions made in public. When people take time to understand those decisions and bring their lived knowledge to the conversation, government becomes more answerable and more capable.

You do not need to arrive with all the answers. Start with curiosity. Read a bill. Check the evidence. Consider who may be affected. Share what you see. Listen for what another community knows. Democracy does not ask us to erase disagreement; it asks us to turn disagreement into a more honest search for common ground.

This workspace is built for that work. [Bring your voice to the main website](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/), and help make public decisions more thoughtful, transparent, and worthy of the people they serve.

## Start here

1. [Review a bill](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/bills) and its linked evidence.
2. [Understand local impact](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/impact) with a district profile.
3. [Compare compromise options](https://governanceapp.ai-aarti.com/compromise) across shared benefit, minimum support, and delivery risk.
