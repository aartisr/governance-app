# Community Code of Conduct

Civic Accord welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds, experiences, and viewpoints. We expect everyone to help keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected conduct

- Be considerate and respectful, including when disagreeing.
- Critique ideas and work rather than attacking people.
- Make room for differing perspectives and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Give feedback that is specific, relevant, and useful.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption, personal attacks, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's GitHub contact options. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports, protect the privacy of people involved, and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary. Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
