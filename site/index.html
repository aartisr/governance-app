The democratic promise

You do not need to agree with everyone to help the country move forward.

Public life asks more of us than a passing reaction. It asks us to stay present: to name what our communities need, to listen across real differences, and to test our opinions against evidence. That kind of participation makes room for stronger laws and a deeper trust between people and the institutions that serve them.

There is no single voice that can carry a democracy. There is the patient, powerful work of many voices choosing to show up.
