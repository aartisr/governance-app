Core capabilities

One workspace, five connected questions

What does the bill say?
Plain section-level bill intelligence, evidence links, status, and budget context.
Who is affected locally?
District-specific estimates with explanations and confidence signals.
What do people care about?
A transparent voice-token model makes preference intensity visible.
Which option works across groups?
Compromise analysis exposes shared benefit and protects minimum support.
How reliable is the context?
Stakeholder trust scores make credibility dimensions explicit.
