Voter Feedback and Trust

On Voter feedback, choose a preset group and adjust its issue sliders. Each issue costs the square of its vote count: two votes cost four tokens. The page displays a 50-token budget but allows overspending. It does not verify people, collect votes, save choices, or change the compromise recommendation.

On Stakeholder trust, sample participants receive a weighted score from four stored attributes: accuracy, expertise, consistency, and transparency. These are illustrative inputs, not measured track records. The scores do not weight amendment utilities.

Democracy is shared work.
