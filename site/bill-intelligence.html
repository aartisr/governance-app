Bill intelligence

How to read a bill with evidence beside it

The Bills workspace is the starting point for a review. It keeps sponsor, policy domain, status, section count, and linked evidence in one place.

Open Bills to begin. Selecting a bill reveals section-level summaries, affected-population context, budget deltas, and the evidence actually linked to that bill.

Reading a bill responsibly

  • Treat summaries as a navigation aid, not a replacement for statutory text.
  • Check source coverage before treating a model output as settled.
  • Use Impact analysis to test how a specific district profile changes the picture.

Stay close to the evidence.

Read the bill. Compare the links. Check what changes for the people who live with the result.
