Impact Analysis

On Impact, choose a sample bill and district. The app adjusts a stored affected-population value using district rural share, small-business share for tax sections, and income. It displays a formula-based exposure equivalent and a confidence value derived from stored inputs.

These numbers are not calibrated forecasts or causal estimates. The exposure formula can exceed 100%, so it should not be read as a literal share of residents. Compare profiles to understand the model's assumptions, then verify real-world claims outside the app.

The methods guide explains the formula and its limits.
