Prototype guide

How It Works

This is a guide to the current prototype. All displayed civic records and scores are sample data.

StepWhat you can doWhat the result means
BillsSearch three fictional bills and read section summariesA demonstration record, not statutory text
ImpactSwitch among three sample districtsFormula-based exposure equivalent, not a forecast
Voter feedbackAdjust priority slidersA page-local exercise; no vote is collected
CompromiseCompare four preset amendmentsRanking under assumed utilities and risks
TrustInspect sample participant scoresDisplay context; scores do not affect the recommendation

The workflow is exploratory. Feedback does not flow into the amendment calculation, group boundaries are not enforced, and the app does not track enacted outcomes. Read the worked example for more context.
